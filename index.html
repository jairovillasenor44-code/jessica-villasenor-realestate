Understanding the Difference

CMA vs. Formal Appraisal

What Jessica provides
Comparative Market Analysis

A strategic pricing report built from real sales data, active listings, and Jessica's firsthand knowledge of your neighborhood — designed to help you make smart, informed decisions.

  • Free of charge — no strings attached
  • Completed in 2–3 business days
  • Factors in condition, upgrades & location nuance
  • Paired with a personal consultation
  • Ideal for sellers, investors & curious homeowners
Required by lenders
Formal Appraisal

A licensed appraiser's official assessment required during mortgage financing or refinancing. Legally binding, lender-ordered, and based on a standardized methodology.

  • Cost: typically $400–$700
  • Ordered by your lender, not your agent
  • Required for purchase loans & refinancing
  • Takes 1–2 weeks to complete
  • May not reflect current market momentum

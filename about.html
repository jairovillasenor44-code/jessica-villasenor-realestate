About Jessica

A trusted partner through every step.

Jessica Villaseñor is a passionate real estate investor and entrepreneur who began her journey in her early twenties by designing homes. What started as a hands-on learning experience quickly grew into a deep-rooted passion for real estate investing.

Through dedicated mentorship and years of experience, Jessica built and led a successful out-of-state investment team and mastered the buy-and-hold strategy, focusing on creating long-term wealth and financial freedom through real estate.

Today, Jessica's life beautifully blends her roles as a devoted mom to her 12-year-old son and proud dog mom to three pups with her unwavering commitment to real estate. Whether she's analyzing deals, mentoring fellow investors, or guiding clients through the buying and selling process across Northern Nevada, Jessica brings the same investor's eye and personal care to every client she represents.

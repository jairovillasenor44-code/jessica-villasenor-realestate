Common Questions

Answers to what buyers & sellers ask most.

Everything you need to know before buying, selling, or investing in Northern Nevada real estate.

Back to main site
How long does it take to buy a home in the Reno/Sparks area? +
From the first showing to closing, most buyers are in their new home within 30–60 days once under contract. The search phase varies — some clients find the right home quickly, others take a few months. Jessica will help you set realistic expectations from day one based on your price range and must-haves.
Do I need to be pre-approved before we start looking at homes? +
Yes — and it's the single most important step before your first showing. Sellers won't consider offers without it, and it gives you a clear budget so you don't fall in love with a home outside your range. Jessica can connect you with trusted local lenders who close on time and communicate well.
What does it cost to work with Jessica as a buyer? +
In most transactions, the buyer's agent fee is paid by the seller as part of the deal — so there is typically no out-of-pocket cost to you for representation. Jessica will walk you through how buyer agent compensation works before you sign anything.
How should I price my home to sell quickly? +
Pricing is a data-driven conversation. Jessica prepares a detailed Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) using recent sales in your neighborhood, current competition, and market trends. The goal is a price that attracts serious buyers fast — without leaving money on the table.
What should I do to prepare my home before listing? +
Small investments go a long way: fresh paint in neutral tones, deep cleaning, decluttering, and professional photography. Jessica will walk through your home and give you a prioritized list of what matters most — so you spend money where it actually moves the needle.
How does Jessica market a home differently than other agents? +
Every listing is launched on the MLS, the Engel & Völkers global network, social media, and targeted digital campaigns. High-quality photography and compelling listing copy are non-negotiable. Jessica also leverages her buyer network — sometimes your buyer is already in her database before the home goes live.
Can Jessica help if I'm relocating from out of state? +
Absolutely. Relocation is a specialty. Jessica can conduct video tours, advise on neighborhoods remotely, and coordinate the entire process so you feel confident making decisions from afar. Nevada is one of the top relocation destinations in the country — she's helped many clients make the move successfully.
What areas does Jessica serve? +
Jessica primarily works in Reno, Sparks, and Fernley, with extended coverage in Carson City and the Northern Lake Tahoe corridor. If you're unsure whether your area is within her coverage zone, just reach out — she'll be upfront about whether she's the right fit.

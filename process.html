How It Works
Your journey, simplified.

Whether you're buying your first home or ready to sell, Jessica guides you through every step with clarity and care.

For Buyers

Buying with Jessica

01

Consultation & Pre-Approval

We start with a conversation about your goals, timeline, and budget. Jessica connects you with trusted lenders to get pre-approved before you start touring.

02

Home Search & Touring

Jessica curates listings that match your criteria and attends every showing — bringing an investor's eye to evaluate each property's true value.

03

Offer & Negotiation

When you find the one, Jessica crafts a competitive offer backed by market data and negotiates every detail to protect your interests.

04

Inspection to Close

From inspection to the closing table, Jessica coordinates every detail so you can focus on what matters — your new home.

For Sellers

Selling with Jessica

01

Home Valuation & Strategy

A detailed comparative market analysis and tailored pricing strategy ~ built to maximize your sale price in today's market.

02

Prepare & Stage

Staging guidance, targeted improvements, and professional photography ~ to make your home stand out on every platform.

03

Listing & Marketing

Your home is launched on MLS, the Engel & Völkers global network, social media, and targeted digital campaigns reaching qualified buyers fast.

04

Negotiate & Close

Every offer is reviewed, the strongest terms are negotiated, and the full closing process is managed ~ stress-free, from contract to keys.

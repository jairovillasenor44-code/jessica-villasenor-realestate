Everything you want to know about home valuations.

A home appraisal is a formal assessment ordered by a lender and conducted by a licensed appraiser — required during financing. A home valuation (CMA) is performed by your real estate agent using recent comparable sales, active listings, and local market conditions. Jessica's valuations are free, faster, and give you the strategic pricing insight you need before you even decide to list.

Online estimates can be off by 5–20% or more, especially in fast-moving markets like Reno. They rely on public data and algorithms that can't account for your home's condition, specific location, recent renovations, or neighborhood nuances. Jessica reviews each property personally and layers in local knowledge that no algorithm can replicate — giving you a number you can actually act on.
